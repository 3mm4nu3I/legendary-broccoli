# Гудендах · Gudendah

**West Flemish, written in Cyrillic.** An alphabet for a dialect that never had one.

Gudendah spells the Ypres–Kortrijk variety of West Flemish (West-Vlaams) phonetically in Ukrainian Cyrillic. It is meant to be learned and taught out loud: read it aloud and you hear the dialect.

## What's inside

- **Why Ukrainian** – the sounds, and solidarity

- **The keyboard** – the Ukrainian phone layout, and how to add it
- **Phrasebook** – everyday West-Vlaams in Cyrillic
- **Learn & teach** – how to pick it up by ear

## Why Ukrainian Cyrillic?

**The sound.** Ukrainian **Г** is a voiced h, exactly the West Flemish g. Its **е**, **и** and **і** match our e, short i and ie.

**Solidarity.** West Flanders knows what it means to be invaded and to have your language pushed aside. So does Ukraine. Writing our dialect in their letters is a small gesture: two peoples who kept talking in their own language, side by side.

## Rule zero

**Spelling is free. Read it aloud.** If it sounds right, it is right.
