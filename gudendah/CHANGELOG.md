# Gudendah – changelog

## Leerversie Nº 001 · 2026-10-24

First public learner's edition.

- **Added** `Gudendah_leerversie_PUBLIC.pdf`: 7-page A5 learner's edition, free to share.
  - Why Ukrainian: the sound match (Ukrainian Г = West Flemish g) and solidarity between West Flanders and Ukraine.
  - The letters: 25 letters in three groups, with their Greek roots.
  - The sound rules, including rule zero: spelling is free, read it aloud.
  - The Ukrainian keyboard layout and how to add it to a phone.
  - Phrasebook and a learn-and-teach page.
- **Added** `README.md` describing the project.
- **Not included**: the private field edition (handshake, rotation calendar, place codes). It stays offline by design.
